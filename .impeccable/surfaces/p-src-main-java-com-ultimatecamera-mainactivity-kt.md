# Camera capture surface

- **Scope / mode:** Android camera viewfinder and capture controls; Operate.
- **Audience and task:** Phone photographers compose a scene, choose a supported capture mode, and take a photo or record video.
- **Primary action:** Capture immediately; in Video mode, start or stop recording.
- **States:** Permission request, ready preview, active timer, recording, unsupported hardware, and capture/configuration failure.
- **Constraints:** Keep the scene visible, keep capture controls reachable, adapt modes to the active camera, and never imply unsupported processing.
- **Direction:** A calibrated camera instrument in obsidian, satin brass, pearl, and recording red; scene-first preview with a compact control deck.
- **Memorable moment:** The shutter changes from a precise still-photo disc to a clear recording/stop state without leaving the viewfinder.
- **Unresolved:** Exact modes and profiles depend on the attached phone's Camera2 capabilities.
